Design leadership

Booking.com Flights design vision

Booking.com

Business goal

We shipped an MVP Flights product in 2019. Beyond a functional booking experience, we needed to learn what our users value to create a truly unique, revenue-driving product.

Objective

Deliver high-fidelity designs in a vision document that is actionable, feasible and has buy-in from stakeholders across the business.

Objectives and scoping

Goal: create a shared understanding of the project.

  • Presented to Flights leadership to align on expectations and timelines.
  • Facilitated a kick-off to share insights and build excitement.

Outcome: a timeline, a list of deliverables and three objectives within scope.

Scoping and project definitions were a vital step. Beginning with clarity ensured that:

  1. leadership were invested
  2. we kept a measured focus on the right topics throughout.

This was a win for the product, because all output from the project had value. And it was a win for the design team, because the work they put in was guaranteed a place on our roadmap. That means morale!

Discovery

Goal: understand user needs, share existing knowledge and facilitate a brainstorm we could shortlist as the foundation of the project.

  • Our UX researcher conducted a worldwide study, which I helped to write.
  • Gathered existing internal learnings, and facilitated meetings to learn together.
  • Ran competitor analysis, assigning each person a competitor to analyse and report back on.
  • Facilitated a department- and craft-agnostic brainstorm.
  • Shortlisted ideas from the brainstorm to 16 final ideas.
  • Presented to leadership for approval before moving into design.

Outcome: 16 product feature ideas to design.

Design

Goal: create technically feasible, high-fidelity designs backed by design reviews.

  • Each content designer and designer pair collaborated on and owned 2–3 features.
  • They were responsible for discovering technical feasibility and dependencies.
  • They presented drafts for feedback each week in our department-wide UX reviews.
  • Once finalised, designs were collated into a vision document, which we presented back to the Flights department.

Results

We published our vision document detailing every design, backed by insights, a Figma link and a named point of contact for each topic.

Impact

  • The vision document was shared widely, letting other departments collaborate and build on our ideas.
  • 12+ of the features have been explored, implemented and/or iterated upon.
