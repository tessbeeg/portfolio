UX writing

Signing up events at Eventix

Eventix

Client

Eventix (now Weeztix)

The challenge

As the only UX writer supporting the start-up, I needed to advocate for UX writing and demonstrate its value every step of the way.

Setting

On-site, working closely with product, partner services and design

Eventix asked me to help with UX writing and design for both their consumer and partner experiences. It was an on-site project where I worked closely with product, partner services and design to build user experiences ready for scale.

My work

I established a tone of voice, built a taxonomy of events terminology, and created a process to localise content and brief translation teams.

Together with a designer, we built the dashboard for partners, helping them create events, customise tickets and opt in to partner services such as TicketSwap and cancellation insurance. I also wrote customer-facing ticket emails and other key messages for the site.
