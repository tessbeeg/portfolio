Notification design Booking.com

Booking.com price alerts

Business goal
Increase revenue and customer retention for the Flights business.
Objective
Deliver valuable price alerts to customers who are flexible and price sensitive.
Metrics
App downloads, conversion, push notification opens, repeat bookings.
Content design goal
Clearly explain price fluctuations through concise push notifications, and make the feature intuitive to discover and use.

Discovery

The challenge

Customers often look for many months before booking a flight. Booking.com’s Flights product wasn’t equipped to help with this behaviour.

Who are our users?

Along with UXR, we sent a global survey that helped us build personas. The trend seeker was a persona with a long booking window, looking for reassurance that they were finding the best possible price. Typically, this user checked the site again and again. This helped inform our UX decisions.

Impact

  • More than 10% of people with a price alert subscription go on to make a booking.
  • Subscribers are much more likely to book than non-subscribers.
  • Users with subscriptions conclusively get a cheaper flight price 🎉

Process

With an understanding of our users and their problems, we built up a concept. Through brainstorms and drafting sessions, we reached early design ideas for price alerts.

Setting the scope

As a team, we agreed on the minimum scope for a push notification flow: telling users when a price rises, drops or is cheaper on an alternative date (matching the trend seeker’s behaviour).
